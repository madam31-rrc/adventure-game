# Adventure Game Plan

## Story Overview
The player embarks on a journey through a mystical forest, where they encounter 
different challenges.

## Story Paths
Path 1: The Lost Wanderer
  - Decision 1: Follow the sound of running water.
  - Decision 2: Enter a mysterious cave.

Path 2: The Cursed Village
  - Decision 1: Speak to the village elder.
  - Decision 2: Search the abandoned house.

Path 3: The Ancient Temple
  - Decision 1: Solve the puzzle at the entrance.
  - Decision 2: Take an alternate route through the jungle.

## Game Features
- Users make choices to navigate the adventure.
- Choices lead to different story outcomes.
- Simple UI with buttons for choices.
- JavaScript handles the story logic.
